Features

Built for the sums you do every day

Everything here is in the current release and runs on your phone.

A calculator that keeps up

Standard and scientific keys: trigonometric, inverse and hyperbolic functions, logarithms, roots, powers, factorials, percentages and memory. Decimal arithmetic avoids the rounding slips of ordinary floating point, and a large backspace key fixes typos fast.

All your currencies at once

Keep a list of the currencies you use, chosen from more than 150. Type an amount into any of them and every other row converts as you type. Tap a currency at zero and simply start typing: the first digit replaces the zero.

Your base currency, remembered

The converter reopens with the base currency you last used. If you allow it, approximate location can pick your country's currency for you, and follow you when you travel. Manual choice always works, with or without location.

Rates shown in full

Exchange rates keep the precision of their source, such as 1 USD = 129.836385 KES, rather than a rounded 130. Each rate set shows when it was updated. Rates refresh over the internet and are saved for offline use.

30 tools in one app

Unit conversion across 16 kinds of measurement, loans and compound interest, VAT and discounts, tip splitting, dates and time zones, BMI, statistics, geometry, a programmer calculator and more. Search finds any of them by name.

Private and yours

No account, no ads, no analytics. History and settings stay on your phone, and you can export them to a backup file and import it again on a new phone.

About exchange rates: rates are indicative reference rates from public rate services, not the rates a bank or bureau will offer you. Refreshing them needs an internet connection; offline, the app uses the last rates it saved, or a built-in reference table that it labels as such.

Install in four steps

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps ("Install unknown apps"). You can switch this off again afterwards.
  4. Install, then open Baziq Calculator from your home screen.

Updating: install the new APK over the old one. Your history and settings stay, because every release is signed with the same key.

Privacy

What the app does with your data

The short version. The privacy page has the details.

  • On your phone. Calculation history, your currency list and settings are stored on the phone. There is no account and no Baziq server.
  • Exchange rates. To refresh rates the app downloads them from public rate services (open.er-api.com, with api.frankfurter.dev as a fallback). Those requests contain the base currency, not your amounts.
  • Location, only if you allow it. Approximate location is used to find the country you are in and set its currency. Android's geocoding service turns the position into a country, which may involve a network request by that service. Only the country code is kept.
  • No tracking. No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries are included.

Support

Help with installing and using the app

Android says the app is from an unknown source

Apps installed outside Google Play need your permission. When asked, allow the app you downloaded with (usually your browser) to install apps, then go back and tap Install. Google Play Protect may also ask you to confirm: the app is not on Google Play, so Play Protect does not know it yet.

"App not installed" or "package conflicts with an existing package"

Another copy of the app, signed with a different key, is already on the phone. Official releases are all signed with the same key (the fingerprint is under "Verify the download"). If you have an unofficial copy, export a backup in Settings first, then uninstall it and install the official APK.

Exchange rates are not updating

Refreshing needs an internet connection. Tap the refresh button in the converter; the "Updated" time shows how recent the rates are. Without a connection the app keeps using the last rates it saved.

"Could not determine your country"

Finding the country needs location turned on and the phone's geocoding service, which usually needs internet. You can always choose your base currency by tapping it in the converter.

Moving to a new phone

In Settings, use Export backup to save a file with your history, currencies and settings. On the new phone, install the app and use Import backup.
