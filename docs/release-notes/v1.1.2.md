## Baziq Calculator 1.1.2

The first release under the Baziq Calculator name, by Baziq Hue.

### Fixes
- **Typing a new currency amount.** Tapping a currency that shows zero and typing 9 now gives 9, not 0.9. The zero is selected and replaced by the first digit; decimals (9.5, or .5) work as before.
- **Editing an amount.** You can move the cursor inside an amount and change a digit; previously the cursor jumped back to the end.
- **Base currency kept after a restart.** The converter reopens with the base currency you last used instead of USD. Automatic local currency still switches it when you arrive in another country.

### New
- **Backup & Restore** (Settings): export your history, currency list, custom rates and settings to a file, and import it on another phone.
- **New name and icon:** Baziq Calculator, with a calculator-key icon that also supports Android 13 themed icons.

### Install
Download `baziq-calculator.apk` below, open it on your phone and allow your browser to install apps if Android asks. Android 8.0 or later.

Releases from 1.1.2 on are signed with the Baziq Calculator release key and install over each other, keeping your data.
